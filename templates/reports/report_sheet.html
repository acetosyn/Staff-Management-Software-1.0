Generation Rules

Report Sheet Requirements

Rules used by the report generation engine.

Students are loaded by class The report page first fetches students from the selected class arm.
Students can be selected Use checkboxes to generate one or more selected student reports.
CA/Test is compulsory A student must have saved CA/Test records for matching subjects.
Supabase CBT is compulsory Reports are blocked when the selected admission number has no live CBT result.
Subject stats are calculated POS, OUT OF, LOW, HIGH and CLASS AVE are generated subject by subject.
Remarks are editable Teacher remark, principal remark and ratings can be edited before printing.
